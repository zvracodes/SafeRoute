Why this exists

Standard maps route you by distance and traffic. None of them route you by safety.

SDG 5 — Gender Equality

Fear of unsafe streets narrows where women and girls can study, work or simply exist after dark. Naming unsafe spots — and confirming safe ones — turns lived experience into shareable, actionable data.

SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities

City planners rarely have street-level safety data at all, let alone from the people most affected. A live, crowdsourced layer like this can point directly at where lighting, footpaths or patrols are missing.
